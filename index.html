Kulhad Chai, Made The Right Way

Welcome to Tealogy Cafe Moradabad — operated by Highstreet Cafe, serving the authentic Tealogy experience.

Since Tealogy began in 2018, one idea has stayed at its heart: India's love affair with chai deserves to be served the way it was meant to be — in a kulhad, a hand-thrown clay cup that makes every sip earthier, warmer, and a little more nostalgic.

Every kulhad is biodegradable and unique — no two cups are exactly alike, just like no two conversations at our tables. Pair your chai with budget-friendly vegetarian bites, all made fresh when you order.

So take a seat, connect to our WiFi, play a quick game, and enjoy your time while we prepare your meal. ☕
